                                            Hand history & action log

                                              Rules & shuffle

                                              No-limit Texas Hold’em · blinds 5/10 · 1,000 free banana chips · no rake. Raise sizes are your total for this betting round. All-ins create side pots; ties split pots. A sizing preset only fills the input; press Bet or Raise to submit.

                                              Session-only chips have no cash value, purchase, transfer or redemption. They are separate from donation bananas. Local practice has computer opponents, optional auto deal and no turn deadline.

                                              Live tables have human players and an experimental encrypted shuffle checked by every browser. Stay connected through the hand, even after folding. If a required contribution or action does not arrive within 90 seconds of the last table event, the hand cancels and all committed chips return. Reloading loses your private session keys. Stand between hands before leaving the room.

                                              Local practice uses Web Crypto and Fisher–Yates; its hands have no independent proof. Spectators see community and public showdown cards only.

                                              Add an Ooga

                                              2140data hands you the prompt. Paste it to your coding agent.

                                              Add an Ooga to Ooga Booga Land. Create one new file, src/characters/<handle>.js, and change nothing else.

It is a plain IIFE (const BL = window.BL;) calling BL.characters.add({ handle, joined, lastCommit, github, display, look, voice, dress }). joined and lastCommit are Unix seconds; joined orders the roster.

look overrides the hashed skin, hair, fur and height and carries the flags build:"slim", bald, hairless, cleanShaven, noBrow, wideEyes, noPupils, face:"nose"|"smirk"|"beard"|"none", eyeColor with eyeGlow, and hatY in sixteenths of height. voice is { poke, idle: [...] }.

dress hooks build the voxels, each called with the kit k (h, u, rand, P, color(hex), jit, vg, root, parts, eyeCells): torso(k, v), club(k), gear(k), skull(k, v), crown(k, v), eyes(k, v), mark(k, v), hatY(k), headgear(k), extras(k). The head is a 7x6x6 block with the eyes at z=5 and the brow at y=4. Keep accessory builders and their caches in the file, and draw from k.rand in build order.

Then run npm run build and ONLY=characters node test/run.mjs.
